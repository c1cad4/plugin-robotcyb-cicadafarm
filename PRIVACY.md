# Privacy policy

The extension requests public token and pool data from api.geckoterminal.com, api.dexscreener.com and, only as a fallback, frontend-api-v3.pump.fun. Those services receive ordinary network information such as IP address and the requested public token/pool address. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Locally stored data: preferred language, latest market snapshots, update timestamps and at most 120 observed USD price points per token. There is no analytics, telemetry, user account, wallet address collection, browsing-history collection or access to page content. The extension never asks for seed phrases or signs transactions. External market/explorer/GitHub links open only when clicked.

Removing the extension removes its browser-managed local storage. Browser profile backups may retain previous copies. Questions: open an issue in https://github.com/c1cad4/plugin-robotcyb-cicadafarm.
